Start boost

Step 1c1. Hold Space the instant the 2 disappears, and keep holding through GO. This is the one moment in the whole site where an accelerate button exists — Jodi drives with auto-accelerate, so holding it means nothing anywhere else, and everything here.

The bar is the teaching device, not the number. Dark green is a perfect start, light green still boosts, and the marker shows where you actually pressed. Being told “180 ms early” tells you how badly you did; seeing your mark sitting just left of the band tells you which way to move.

Difficulty

Generous is the default and almost certainly what ships. The plan's rule is concepts, not grinding — the drill exists to build the instinct, and the Switch is where the hands get trained. Tight is here to prove the window can be narrowed, not because Jodi should ever meet it.
