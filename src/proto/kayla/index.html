What to feel for

The gate this feeds is 4e3: the gag lands as funny, not mean. Three things to watch.

The first ninety seconds. Beat one is the whole risk. Someone who clicked her own name out of curiosity has to work out, with nothing telling her, that the notice can be taken apart. The full stop sags and falls on a timer whether or not she does anything. Does that read as an invitation, or as a rendering bug?

The hints. The loudest complaint about the game this borrows from is that the narrator solves the puzzles over your shoulder. The ladder here waits 25 seconds, then 60, then 110 before saying the answer outright. Too slow? Too fast?

The last ninety seconds. Chapter K hands her the complete list of what her mum has been taught, as intelligence on an opponent, and asks exactly one thing of her — pick the Mushroom Cup, and do not let her win. Is that warm, or does it tip into the kind of sincerity a fifteen-year-old closes the tab on?
