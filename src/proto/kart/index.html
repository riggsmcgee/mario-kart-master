Drive

← → or A/D to steer. R to reset. There is no accelerator — the kart always drives forward, exactly like Jodi's auto-accelerate setting.

The blue nose is where the kart points. The grey line is where it is actually going. When those two disagree, that gap is the drift.

What to feel for

This is a rehearsal for gate 1b6, so the questions are the same. Is the steering responsive without being twitchy — can you hold a smooth line through the corner, or does it snap? Does the back end stepping out feel like a kart or like ice? Does grass feel like a real penalty without feeling like a punishment? Does hitting the wall cost you something?

Every number below the canvas is live. When something feels wrong, move the slider until it does not, then hit Copy TUNING.md entry and tell me what you changed and why.
