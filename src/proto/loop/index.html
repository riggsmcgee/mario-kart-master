Game loop

Step 1a3. Fixed-timestep sim with an interpolated render. The sim runs at a constant rate no matter what the display does, so a trick window tuned to 90 ms is 90 ms on every machine.

Interpolation

Both pucks run off the same sim. The top one is drawn at the raw sim position; the bottom one is interpolated by alpha. Drag the sim rate down to 10 Hz and watch the top puck stutter while the bottom stays smooth.

Controls

Stalling blocks the main thread, which is what a slow frame really is. The sim catches up to the clamp and discards the rest — watch dropped tick up instead of the page locking into a spiral. Click another window: it pauses on blur and resumes without fast-forwarding.
